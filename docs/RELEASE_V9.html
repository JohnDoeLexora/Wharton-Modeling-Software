v9.0.0 release notes

Exploratory toolkit. This sheet is not a portfolio, a reserve, or a facility gift. Case cash flows stay locked. WInS capital stays $300,000 and does not enter the projection.

What changed

  • Compensated compound-Poisson jumps. No class fallback after three quiet years.
  • Comparison is a paired difference with a paired standard error. The caller's reserve and market drift are kept.
  • A flat trial-curve reserve uses the stated discount yield and warns. Teaching hurdle $439,305.45.
  • ACT/ACT Treasuries, 30/360 corporates, negative accrued interest inside an ex-coupon window.
  • Curve lab: standard error versus N for pseudo-random, antithetic, and Sobol draws, plus a target-SE stop that does not rewrite the form.
  • Effective sample size on mean wealth, P(funded), drawdown, and Sharpe.
